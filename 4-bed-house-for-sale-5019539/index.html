DNG Boyce Gallagher are delighted to present to market this exceptional A1-rated detached family residence situated in the peaceful rural townland of Prucklish, Churchill, Co. Donegal.

Completed in 2025 and finished to an exceptional standard throughout, this impressive four-bedroom family home occupies a beautifully maintained site with a fully concrete driveway and detached garage. Combining contemporary energy-efficient construction with luxurious finishes and elegant design, this remarkable property offers the perfect balance of modern family living and countryside tranquillity.

One of the standout features of this exceptional home is its remarkable energy efficiency. While many modern homes achieve excellent energy ratings, this property goes a step further, combining premium construction standards with carefully considered upgrades to create a home that is as economical to run as it is comfortable to live in. Boasting an A1 BER rating, the property benefits from an air-to-water heating system, underfloor heating throughout, triple-glazed windows and exceptional insulation levels. Further enhancing its credentials, an 18-panel photovoltaic (PV) solar array was installed in 2025 and has already surpassed its projected annual energy output within the first twelve months of operation. The result is a future-proofed family home offering outstanding year-round comfort, significantly reduced energy costs and a level of efficiency rarely found, even among newly constructed homes.

From the moment you enter, the quality of finish is immediately apparent. A welcoming entrance hall with engineered timber flooring leads to a superb open-plan kitchen, dining and sunroom area which forms the heart of the home. This bright and expansive living space has been thoughtfully designed for both everyday family life and entertaining, with large windows capturing natural light and framing views of the surrounding countryside.

The bespoke kitchen is fitted with an extensive range of premium cabinetry, quartz worktops, pantry storage and a large central island, creating a space that is both highly functional and beautifully finished. Premium Bosch appliances include a double eye-level oven together with a Bosch venting induction hob featuring integrated downdraft extraction. The dining area flows seamlessly into the sunroom, where an impressive double-fronted solid fuel stove with granite hearth and stone surround sits centrally between the two spaces, creating a striking focal point while allowing both rooms to enjoy its warmth and ambience.

A beautifully appointed living room offers a peaceful escape from the hub of the home, enjoying far reaching views across the Donegal landscape. Generous proportions, quality finishes and an energy-efficient wood pellet stove combine to create a warm and relaxing space.

Accommodation is generously proportioned throughout and includes four spacious double bedrooms, including a luxurious master suite with built-in wardrobes and luxurious ensuite bathroom including a jacuzzi bath. The home's four bathrooms have all been finished to an exceptional standard with contemporary tiling, premium sanitary ware, quality shower installations and heated towel radiators.

Externally, the detached garage provides excellent storage and workshop space and has been fully insulated, offering year-round comfort and enhanced versatility. The substantial first-floor level provides superb flexibility and is ideally suited as a home office, studio, gym, hobby room or additional accommodation, complete with shower room facilities.

The property enjoys a highly sought-after setting in the Churchill area, one of Donegal's most scenic locations. Renowned for its strong community spirit and spectacular surroundings, Church Hill sits on the edge of Glenveagh National Park, offering residents unrivalled access to mountains, forests, lakes, walking trails and some of the most breathtaking landscapes in Ireland.

The vibrant town of Letterkenny is less than 20 minutes away. As the commercial, educational and healthcare hub of the Northwest, Letterkenny is home to major employers including Optum, Tata Consultancy Services, Pramerica, Atlantic Technological University (ATU) and Letterkenny University Hospital (LUH).

Offering the rare combination of peaceful countryside living, exceptional energy efficiency and outstanding interior specification, this magnificent residence is presented in true turnkey condition. Homes of this calibre rarely come to market and early viewing with sole selling agents DNG Boyce Gallagher is highly recommended.
